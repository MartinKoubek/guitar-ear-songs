# Level 0: Before You Play

Approved 18 November 2026. A short introduction before Level 1, not a replacement for the eight Hot Cross Buns lessons. All content is English. Only assets/ is distributed.

## Lesson map

1. Hold Your Guitar: comfortable support and relaxed shoulders; open high E as one selectable untimed target. Try three separate plucks manually.
2. Your First Plucks: relaxed right forearm/hand near the sound hole, finger towards the palm; the same open E target, repeated manually.
3. Your First Fretted Note: open E and high E fret 2, selected manually in the existing untimed picker. Try 0, 2, 0 without a timer. No fourth-fret stretch, bass or chord task.

The score contains distinct targets rather than duplicate target buttons: one target in lessons 1/2, two in lesson 3. Three attempts are an instruction, not an automatically counted achievement. Microphone feedback judges pitch only, not posture or finger placement. Each JSON retains two real count-in bars and an explicit silent aligned backing; defaults are None and previewMelody true. Artwork intentionally uses the existing shared default-cover.webp temporarily; it is decorative, not a posture diagram.
