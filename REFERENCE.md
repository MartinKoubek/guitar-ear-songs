# Hot Cross Buns reference

Preserve the familiar melody and introduce one new skill per lesson. The actual four-bar tune is G#4/F#4/E4 (quarter/quarter/half), repeat; four E4 eighths then four F#4 eighths; finish G#4/F#4/E4 (quarter/quarter/half).

1. **Right Hand & Rhythm:** open high-e preparation with rests,60BPM.
2. **First Fretted Notes:** untimed high-e frets0/2/4, manual target choice and microphone feedback.
3. **First Musical Phrase:** high-e4/2/0 motif with preparation rests,60BPM.
4. **The Quicker Middle:** repeated eighths with rest bars,60BPM.
5. **The Complete Tune:** complete four-bar tune repeated three times,64BPM.
6. **First Bass:** thumb plays separate open low E before melody,50BPM.
7. **Alternating Bass:** melody stays on high e; selected phrase downbeats pair it with alternating open low E (E2) and A-string fret2 (B2),50BPM.
8. **Melody Across Strings:** keep the same pitches as lesson7. G#4 stays high-e4 and F#4 stays high-e2; only E4 moves to B-string fret5, exactly the same pitch as open high e. Practise without bass, repeat motifs with rests, then play the complete tune twice with familiar E/B bass,45BPM.

The eighth lesson is intentionally a small step from the seventh. Do not replace it with the deferred lower-octave D/G study. The catalog currently contains only these eight lessons, in Level1 / Hot Cross Buns; levels2-15 are empty placeholders.

All lessons contain two real empty count-in bars. Unknown notes are yellow, confirmed correct notes green, wrong notes red. Every written note contributes to the result denominator; simultaneous bass and melody receive independent results. Main backing never substitutes for the learner's bass. Instructional catalog previews may explicitly demonstrate written learner notes.
