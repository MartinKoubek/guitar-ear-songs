# Amazing Grace - Level 1 accompaniment

Approved source: Relax and Learn Guitar, Beginner's Guide to Fingerstyle Guitar: Amazing Grace Made EASY, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6AMGNWBoHcc (2024-04-05; pattern at2:37, performance at4:53). Companion teacher sheet: https://relaxandlearnguitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Amazing-Grace-Fingerstyle-tab.pdf. This is a newly encoded beginner accompaniment curriculum, not a copied graphical score or an arrangement combining the sung melody with bass.

Place after Quiet Morning in Level1. Four lessons at48BPM in3/4. Two explicit count-in bars each. The right-hand pattern is six separate eighths: bass/G/B/G/e/B, p-i-m-i-a-m. Do not carry over Quiet Morning's simultaneous bass+G start or its quarter-note opening.

1. Pattern on G: two bars then a full rest bar, three attempts.
2. G and C: C alone twice with rest bars, then G-G-C-G three times, with preparation rests.
3. G and D: D alone twice with rest bars, then G-G-D-D three times, with preparation rests.
4. Complete accompaniment: G-G-C-G / G-G-D-D / G-G-C-G / G-D-G-G, twice, with one rest bar between rounds and at the end.

Partial G uses lowE3/highE3, G/B open; partial C uses A3/B1, G/e open. D uses D0/G2/B3/e2. Only frets0-3. No barre, stretch to fret4/5, or simultaneous learner notes. Chord names show actual held shapes in all four lessons. Scores explicitly leave main backing silent; previewMelody demonstrates the written guitar accompaniment.
